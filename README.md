# 🌱 Roots & Canopy - Habit Tracker

A gamified habit tracker that helps you grow your habits like a tree.

## 🎯 About

Roots & Canopy is a personal growth app that turns your daily habits into a visual journey. Plant goals, grow trees, and watch your progress bloom.

### The Metaphor

- **Goals** = Mountains you're climbing toward
- **Trees** = Major milestones (stations) on your path
- **Habits** = Water droplets that make trees grow
- **You** = The forest

## ✨ Features

- 🎯 **Long-term goals** with progress tracking
- 🌳 **Trees (milestones)** with visual growth stages
- 💧 **Habits** (minimum 3 per tree) that water your trees
- ✅ **Daily tracking** with checkboxes
- 💭 **Reason logging** for missed habits (learn from setbacks)
- 📊 **30-day history** view
- 🏆 **Points and levels** system
- 🔥 **Streak tracking** for consistency
- 🗑️ **Delete functionality** for goals, trees, and habits
- 📱 **Mobile responsive** design

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Backend:** Flask (Python)
- **Database:** SQLite + SQLAlchemy
- **Authentication:** Flask-Login
- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- **Templating:** Jinja2
